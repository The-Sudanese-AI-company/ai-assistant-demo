# Questions for ONB — information the agent still needs

The knowledge base covers what ONB publishes. These answers only ONB can give; until then the agent refers customers to 5656 or the branch. Ordered by how often customers are likely to ask.

## High priority
1. **Fees and charges** — account fees, card issuance/replacement, transfers (internal, to other banks via BBAN, international), cheque books, Banki-net, statements.
2. **Lost or stolen card** — the exact procedure and whether 5656 can block a card 24/7.
3. **Working hours** — branch hours, Saturday opening, and 5656 call-center hours.
4. **Branches today** — the full, current list of open branches, Bint Khuwailed sub-branches, agencies and windows, with locations; and which branches are temporarily hosted elsewhere (as with General Command and Mohamed Naguib at Obeid Khatim).
5. **O-Cash limits** — daily and per-transaction limits for internal, mobile-number, BBAN and QR transfers; what to do when the app locks after failed logins.
6. **SMS banking** — the short code and command syntax (balance, mini statement, transfer), and how to subscribe.

## Products
7. **Financing** — eligibility, documents, profit margins, maximum amounts and tenors for Murabaha, Ijara, Istisnaa/construction, Umrahtak Alina, Afaaq and microfinance; which branches offer each.
8. **Investment deposits** — minimum amount, terms, last declared profit-distribution ratios, early-withdrawal rules.
9. **Foreign-currency accounts** — documents, currencies accepted today, deposit/withdrawal rules.
10. **Visa prepaid card** — how to apply, fees, how to reload, whether it can be issued remotely.
11. **International transfers** — confirm SWIFT/BIC (OMDBSDKH), account/BBAN format for incoming transfers, correspondent banks, documents for outgoing transfers.
12. **Corporate** — documents to open a company account; what "solutions and facilitations" covers.

## Operations
13. **ATM network** — current number and locations of working ATMs.
14. **O-Cash on iPhone** — which App Store listing is the official one.
15. **Complaints** — expected response time and whether customers get a reference number.
16. **Remote account opening** — whether the online flow is still active and its current URL.
